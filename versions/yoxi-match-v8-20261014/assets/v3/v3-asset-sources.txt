IMAGE TREATMENT AND TRUTH
The downloaded JPEG originals are unchanged: no pixel editing, generation, retouching, color grading, resizing or destructive crop. The application uses CSS object-fit/object-position to crop images to different card shapes; overlays and darkening are display layers. The original SVG poster is a separate project-created vector asset.
The three Taipei photos illustrate interests and quiz choices. They are not photos taken by the fictional member 小安, evidence of the fictional 2026-10-17 event, or current business listings. Their original photographers and licenses are credited on references.html. None establishes a sponsor or photographer endorsement.
